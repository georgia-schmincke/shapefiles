# Shapefiles

Temporary hosting of geojson files.

The files in this repo are from https://geoportal.statistics.gov.uk/, provided by the Office for National Statistics, and from https://www.nisra.gov.uk/, provided by the Northern Ireland Statistics and Research Agency.
